# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NuxOffice is built by people from many countries, languages and backgrounds. We want everyone who participates, in code, design, documentation, issues, reviews or discussions, to feel welcome and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their footing.
- Write in a way that non-native English speakers can follow. Patience with language is part of being inclusive.
- Respect differing opinions on technical direction; resolve disagreements with evidence, ADRs and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Insults, demeaning comments, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews or community spaces.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel representing the project.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers through GitHub (direct message to a maintainer or the repository's private reporting features). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
